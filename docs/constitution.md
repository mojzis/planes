# Paper planes

## Purpose

Give a kid who just started folding planes that are easy enough to fold alone and
a little extravagant: fins, trim flaps, lock flaps, a tube.

## Goals

- One page per plane: the steps, a 3D picture of the finished plane, two PDFs.
- Steps that work without a printer: sizes in cm, one plain A4 sheet.
- Pictures generated from the fold model, never drawn by hand, so they match.
- Later: a page where you change a plane's properties (wing width, nose, flaps) and
  see the result, and fold animations.

## Non-goals

- Copies of well-known existing models or their names.
- Colour decoration for now: line style only.

## Principles

- The fold model is the single source for diagrams, 3D views and line sheets.
- Each step does one thing and says it in one or two plain sentences.
- Field reports from real folding win over theory (stabilizers are standard because
  they helped).
